##🎬 My Favorite Movie: *Kimi no Na wa* (Your Name)

![Kimi no Na wa Poster](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/en/0/0b/Your_Name_poster.png)

---

###Overview
*Kimi no Na wa* (Your Name) is a 2016 **Japanese animated romantic fantasy** film  
written and directed by **Makoto Shinkai**.  

It tells the story of **two teenagers**, *Mitsuha Miyamizu* and *Taki Tachibana*,  
who mysteriously begin to **swap bodies** despite living in different parts of Japan —  
and possibly, **different timelines**.

---

### Main Characters
| Character | Voice Actor | Description |

### Why I Love It
1. The **animation** is absolutely breathtaking — every frame looks like a painting.  
2. The **storyline** beautifully mixes *romance, mystery,* and *fantasy*.  
3. The **soundtrack** by *RADWIMPS* perfectly enhances the emotions.  
4. It explores **fate, time, and connection** in such a heartfelt way.  
5. The ending is **bittersweet yet deeply satisfying**.
